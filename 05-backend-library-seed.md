# 05 — Backend Library Seed

This is the comprehensive backend library to load on first run: every element the team can place on a client board, plus the industry templates. No single client sees all of it. Default text is a starting point the team edits per client. Keep defaults short and honest, in Distl's voice.

The human-readable library is below. The machine-loadable JSON seed is at the end.

## The five columns (domains)

| slug | name | outcome line |
|---|---|---|
| be-findable | Be Findable | people searching for what you sell find you, not your competitor |
| be-there | Be There When Intent Is High | when someone is ready to buy, you're in front of them |
| be-known | Be Known and Trusted | you know your differentiators and they show everywhere |
| be-worth-choosing | Be Worth Choosing | you turn the people you've earned into enquiries |
| be-remembered | Be Remembered | you stay in front of the people who aren't ready yet |

## Library elements

### Be Findable
1. **Rank for your most profitable searches** — recommend: SEO retainer, tier one — why: People searching for what you sell should find you, not a competitor. Right now you likely rank for your name but not the searches that actually bring enquiries. — examples: Priority keyword targeting, service and content pages, on-page optimisation, internal linking.
2. **Own your local map** — recommend: Local SEO and Google Business Profile management — why: Most local buying decisions start with the map. If you're not in the top three for your area, you're invisible at the moment people choose. — examples: Google Business Profile optimisation, review strategy, local citations, location pages.
3. **Be visible in AI search** — recommend: Generative Engine Optimisation — why: More people get answers from AI tools before they ever click a link. If your business isn't referenced, you miss a growing slice of discovery. — examples: Content structured for AI answers, entity and schema work, authority signals.
4. **Earn the authority that makes you the trusted name** — recommend: Link building and digital PR — why: Google trusts sites that reputable sites point to. Without earned links you'll struggle to outrank established competitors. — examples: Digital PR placements, quality backlinks, niche directories, partnerships.
5. **Protect rankings with a clean technical foundation** — recommend: Technical SEO — why: A slow, broken or poorly structured site quietly bleeds rankings no matter how good the content is. — examples: Site speed, crawl and index health, structured data, mobile performance.
6. **Move your site without losing rankings** — recommend: SEO-managed website migration — why: A migration done wrong can wipe years of ranking equity overnight. — examples: Redirect mapping, pre and post-launch audits, indexation monitoring.
7. **Recover from a ranking drop or penalty** — recommend: Penalty recovery — why: If traffic has fallen off a cliff, you need to diagnose and fix the cause before anything else works. — examples: Manual action review, toxic link audit, recovery plan.

### Be There When Intent Is High
1. **Capture the buyers searching right now** — recommend: Google Ads search, tier one — why: When someone searches with intent, you want to be there. Search ads put you at the top the moment they're ready. — examples: Search campaigns, keyword and negative management, conversion tracking, ad copy.
2. **Put your products in front of ready buyers** — recommend: Google Shopping management — why: For product businesses, Shopping ads show your item, price and image right at the point of decision. — examples: Merchant Centre setup, feed optimisation, Shopping and Performance Max.
3. **Win back the ones who didn't convert** — recommend: Remarketing — why: Most people don't buy on the first visit. Remarketing keeps you in front of the ones who showed interest. — examples: Audience setup, display and social remarketing, dynamic ads.
4. **Reach buyers before they search** — recommend: Paid social (Meta), tier one — why: Not everyone is searching yet. Paid social puts you in front of the right people while they scroll, building demand. — examples: Meta campaigns, audience targeting, creative testing, conversion tracking.
5. **Reach decision-makers on LinkedIn** — recommend: LinkedIn Ads — why: For B2B and considered purchases, LinkedIn targets by role, industry and company like nothing else. — examples: Sponsored content, lead gen forms, audience targeting.
6. **Reach a visual, discovery-led audience** — recommend: Pinterest Ads — why: For homewares, design, weddings and lifestyle, Pinterest reaches people actively planning purchases. — examples: Pin campaigns, audience targeting, shopping integration.
7. **Build category awareness at scale** — recommend: Programmatic advertising — why: When you need broad, targeted reach beyond search and social, programmatic places you across the web efficiently. — examples: Display, video, audience buys, brand placements.
8. **Stay in their ears with audio** — recommend: Spotify advertising — why: Audio reaches people in moments other channels can't, building familiarity in your market. — examples: Audio ads, targeting by audience and location.

### Be Known and Trusted
1. **Know why you, not them** — recommend: Brand strategy — why: If you can't say clearly why a customer should choose you, your marketing has nothing solid to stand on. — examples: Positioning, differentiators, messaging framework, audience definition.
2. **Look the tier you want to compete at** — recommend: Brand identity and guidelines, tier one — why: If your identity reads a tier below where you want to sit, it undercuts your pricing and pulls down everything else. — examples: Logo system, colour and type, brand guidelines, application templates.
3. **Have a logo that carries the brand** — recommend: Logo design — why: Your logo is the most repeated asset you own. It needs to work everywhere and signal the right thing. — examples: Primary and secondary logos, file formats, usage rules.
4. **Keep design consistent everywhere** — recommend: Graphic design support — why: Inconsistent design across touchpoints makes even good businesses look smaller and less trustworthy. — examples: Templates, collateral, social and print assets, ongoing design.
5. **Sound like you everywhere** — recommend: Tone of voice guide — why: A consistent voice makes you recognisable and trusted across every channel and every writer. — examples: Voice principles, do and don't examples, channel guidance.

### Be Worth Choosing
1. **An industry-leading website that earns instant credibility** — recommend: Website design and build (WordPress), tier one — why: Your website is where interest becomes an enquiry. If it's slow, dated or hard to use, you lose people at the last step. — examples: Custom design, mobile-first build, clear journeys, fast load.
2. **A high-performance custom build** — recommend: React website development — why: For complex or high-traffic needs, a custom build gives you speed, flexibility and a better experience than a template. — examples: Custom front end, integrations, performance optimisation.
3. **A site built to turn visits into enquiries** — recommend: UX and conversion optimisation — why: Traffic is wasted if the site doesn't convert. The journey, proof and calls to action decide whether visits become leads. — examples: UX review, conversion paths, proof and testimonials, testing.
4. **An ecommerce experience that makes buying easy** — recommend: Ecommerce build (Shopify), optimised — why: Every extra step in checkout costs sales. A well-built store removes friction at every stage. — examples: Shopify build, product filtering, streamlined checkout, fast mobile.
5. **Reliable hosting and infrastructure** — recommend: Domains and managed hosting — why: Downtime and slow servers cost you trust and rankings. Solid infrastructure is the floor everything stands on. — examples: Managed hosting, domains, SSL, backups, uptime monitoring.
6. **A custom app where your service needs one** — recommend: App development (web or mobile) — why: Some businesses need a tool, portal or app, not just a site, to deliver their service well. — examples: Web apps, mobile apps, customer portals, integrations.

### Be Remembered
1. **A consistent organic presence on the right channels** — recommend: Organic social management — why: Your audience decides who to trust partly on whether you show up consistently where they already are. — examples: Channel management, weekly content, reels, community management.
2. **A social strategy tied to business goals** — recommend: Social strategy — why: Posting without a strategy is busywork. A clear plan ties social to real outcomes. — examples: Channel plan, content pillars, posting cadence, measurement.
3. **A content engine that feeds every channel** — recommend: Content strategy and marketing — why: One strong content engine feeds search, social and email at once, and earns attention instead of buying all of it. — examples: Content strategy, blogs and articles, reels, repurposing.
4. **Branded content that builds the brand** — recommend: Branded content — why: Content that carries your brand, not just information, is what makes people remember and prefer you. — examples: Brand films, photography, campaign content.
5. **Email marketing working your customer list** — recommend: Email marketing and automation — why: Your customer list is the one audience you own. Email keeps you in front of them without paying for every touch. — examples: Automation flows, newsletters, segmentation, list growth.
